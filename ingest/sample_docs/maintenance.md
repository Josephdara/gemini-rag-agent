# Maintenance

Harbor Cycle Works is a fictional bicycle shop used as sample data for this repository.

## First service

Every new bicycle includes a free first service within 90 days of delivery. Cables stretch and bolts settle during
the first rides, so the service adjusts shifting and brakes and checks every bolt against its torque specification.
Book it at any partner shop listed on our website.

## Routine care

Clean and lubricate the chain every 300 kilometers, or after every wet ride. Check tire pressure weekly; the
recommended range is printed on the tire sidewall. Replace the chain when a chain checker shows 0.5 percent stretch,
which protects the cassette and chainrings from early wear.

## Electric bicycles

Store an electric bicycle battery at 40 to 60 percent charge if you will not ride for more than a month, and keep it
between 10 and 25 degrees Celsius. Never charge a battery below 0 degrees Celsius. Motor firmware updates are
installed during the first service and at any partner shop.
