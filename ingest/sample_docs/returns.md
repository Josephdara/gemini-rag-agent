# Returns and refunds

Harbor Cycle Works is a fictional bicycle shop used as sample data for this repository.

## Return window

You can return an unridden bicycle within 30 days of delivery for a full refund. Accessories, such as helmets,
lights, and locks, can be returned within 60 days if they are in their original packaging.

A bicycle that has been ridden can be returned within 14 days, minus a 15 percent restocking fee. Custom builds and
bicycles fitted with customer-requested parts cannot be returned, but they are still covered by the warranty.

## How to start a return

Start a return from the Orders page of your account and choose a reason. We email a prepaid shipping label within
one business day. Pack the bicycle in the original box if you still have it; otherwise, any local bike shop can pack
it for you, and we refund up to 40 dollars of the packing cost when you send us the receipt.

## Refunds

Refunds go back to the original payment method within 5 business days of the return arriving at our warehouse.
Store credit is issued instantly instead if you choose it when you start the return, and it includes a 10 percent
bonus. Original shipping charges are not refunded unless the item arrived damaged or was not what you ordered.
