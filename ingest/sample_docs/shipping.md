# Shipping

Harbor Cycle Works is a fictional bicycle shop used as sample data for this repository.

## Where we ship

We ship to all 50 US states. We do not ship outside the United States, to PO boxes, or to freight forwarders.

## Delivery times and costs

Bicycles ship from our warehouse in Richmond, Virginia, 90 percent assembled. Standard delivery takes 4 to 7
business days and is free on orders over 500 dollars; below that it costs 49 dollars. Expedited delivery takes 2 to
3 business days and costs 129 dollars for a bicycle or 19 dollars for accessories only.

Orders placed before 1 p.m. Eastern Time on a business day leave the warehouse the same day. You receive a tracking
link by email when the order ships.

## Damaged deliveries

Check the box before you sign for it. If it is visibly damaged, note that on the carrier's delivery receipt, take
photos, and contact us within 48 hours. We send a replacement or a free repair kit, whichever fixes the problem
faster.
