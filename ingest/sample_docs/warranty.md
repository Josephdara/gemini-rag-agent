# Warranty

Harbor Cycle Works is a fictional bicycle shop used as sample data for this repository.

## What is covered

Frames and rigid forks have a lifetime warranty against defects in materials and workmanship for the original
owner. Suspension forks, rear shocks, and electric drive systems are covered for 2 years. All other components,
such as brakes, wheels, and drivetrain parts, are covered for 1 year.

Electric bicycle batteries are covered for 2 years or 500 full charge cycles, whichever comes first. A battery that
holds less than 70 percent of its rated capacity within that period counts as defective.

## What is not covered

The warranty does not cover normal wear, such as tires, brake pads, chains, and cassettes. It also does not cover
damage from crashes, from use in competition, or from modifications made outside an authorized shop.

## Making a claim

Email warranty@harborcycle.example with your order number, photos of the problem, and the frame serial number,
which is stamped under the bottom bracket. We reply within 3 business days. If a claim is approved, we repair or
replace the part at no cost, including shipping both ways.
